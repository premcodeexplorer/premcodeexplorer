## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🌾 [MilletSaarthi — Agentic AI for Millet Farmers](https://milletsaarthi-fdujl29gnyi3zvrjnrmaa4.streamlit.app/)
A farmer uploads a grain photo and gets a predicted quality grade, expected price, and the best nearby mandi to sell at — powered by 4 parallel AI agents (Quality, Price, Market, Decision) in a Planner + Orchestrator pattern. Trained an EfficientNetB0 classifier on a personally collected ~12K image dataset of ragi, bajra, and jowar; price modelling with XGBoost on real AGMARKNET (Govt. of India) data.

### 🌽 [Grain Quality Classification — EfficientNetV2-S](https://github.com/premcodeexplorer/Grain_set)
Trained EfficientNetV2-S on 141K images from GrainSet (Nature Scientific Data, 2023) for 7-class cereal-grain quality detection — **98.98% macro F1, 99.23% accuracy**. Focal Loss, AdamW + cosine-annealing, systematic hyperparameter tuning across 12 configurations.

### 💰 [Adult Census Income Prediction System](https://huggingface.co/spaces/prembaba/income-prediction-app)
Complete ML pipeline on 48,842 samples — EDA, feature engineering, and benchmarking of 6 models. XGBoost hit **87.29% accuracy / 0.93 ROC-AUC**; deployed live on Hugging Face Spaces with a model comparison dashboard. [[Code]](https://github.com/premcodeexplorer/income-prediction-app)
